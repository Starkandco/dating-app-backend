Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24/09/2026

Starkandco operates I'm dating. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information we process

The app may process your GitHub username, authorization information, profile biography, contact text, profile image, gallery images, and content you choose to publish. The app may also process technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you with GitHub, create and update your publishing repository, publish your profile through GitHub Pages, restore your profile on your device, and provide support and security.

Local storage

Profile information and images may be stored locally in the app’s data directory to make the app easier to use. Local data is not automatically encrypted by the app. You can clear local profile data through the app’s delete repo feature.

GitHub

The app uses GitHub’s APIs and GitHub Pages. Information sent to or stored by GitHub is handled according to GitHub’s own policies. Please review GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Terms of Service at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies.

Public publishing

Content you publish may be publicly visible on GitHub and GitHub Pages. Do not publish information that you want to keep private. Removing content later may not remove copies that have been downloaded, cached, forked, or retained by others.

Authorization tokens

GitHub authorization data is used only to perform the actions you request. Access tokens are managed by our backend service and are not shared with other users. We do not persist GitHub access tokens in local profile files.

Deletion

You may clear locally stored profile data through the app. No data is controlled by us personally, all data is held locally or on GitHub. Deleting a GitHub repository does not delete your GitHub account. To delete your GitHub account, use GitHub’s account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted at https://github.com/starkandco/i-m-dating/ with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to im.dating.app@gmail.com.
