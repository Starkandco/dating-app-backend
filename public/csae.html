Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Our Commitment

I'm dating has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, trafficking, or any activity that harms or endangers children.

We are committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations.

Prohibited Content and Behaviour

Users may not use I'm dating to:

  • Create, publish, share, promote, or distribute child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Groom, exploit, coerce, or sexually solicit minors.
  • Facilitate child trafficking or exploitation.
  • Encourage, depict, or promote the sexual abuse of children.
  • Use the platform in any way that endangers children.

Any content or behaviour that violates these standards may be removed immediately, and associated accounts or hosted profiles may be suspended or permanently banned.

Reporting Concerns

Published GitHub Profiles

Concerns about a profile published through I'm dating can be reported using the Report button on the published profile. This allows users to report the profile to the app developer by email. GitHub-hosted profiles may also be reported through GitHub's reporting mechanisms when applicable.

In-App Contact Form

Users can submit child safety concerns, abuse reports, or other safety-related issues using the contact form button in the app.

Email Contact

Child safety concerns can also be reported directly to im.dating.app@gmail.com.

Review and Enforcement

Reports are reviewed promptly. If we identify content that violates these standards, we may:

  • Remove the content.
  • Disable access to the profile.
  • Suspend or terminate access to the service.
  • Preserve evidence as required by law.
  • Report illegal activity to appropriate authorities where legally required.

Cooperation with Authorities

We will cooperate with law enforcement and relevant child protection organisations regarding confirmed cases of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Child Safety Contact

For matters related to child safety, CSAE, or CSAM, contact I'm dating at im.dating.app@gmail.com.

Scope

These standards apply to all content, profiles, and interactions facilitated through I'm dating, regardless of whether the content is hosted directly by us or published through third-party services such as GitHub Pages.
